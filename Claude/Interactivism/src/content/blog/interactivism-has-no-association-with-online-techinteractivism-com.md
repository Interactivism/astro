A fraudulent website is impersonating Interactivism's name, branding, and content. **We have no affiliation with online-techinteractivism.com whatsoever.**

Our options for action are limited since the scam site is registered and hosted in China.

## How the scam operates

- Victims receive payment offers in Tether (USDT) cryptocurrency for completing small tasks labeled "optimizing apps"
- Commission payments are promised for submitted tasks
- The scheme escalates through "combination tasks" requiring deposits of additional USDT to clear negative balances
- Initial deposits are small but increase progressively
- Communication occurs via WhatsApp

## What to do

If you've encountered a website impersonating Interactivism, please report it to us at hello@interactivism.com.

If you're a victim of this type of scam, we recommend contacting Alibaba Cloud (the registrar) through their social media channels on Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, and TikTok.

The real Interactivism is a product design and development studio based in Pasadena, CA. We do not solicit work through WhatsApp, offer cryptocurrency payment tasks, or operate any website other than interactivism.com.
