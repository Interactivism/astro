Reframe is based on the idea of a salon: a gathering of astute, cross-functional, and interdisciplinary thinkers to discuss topics around technology and design. We felt compelled to examine the tech industry more carefully given its rapid evolution and the significant changes ahead.

This edition's theme: **Reframe: What's Next?**

What should technology professionals and designers prioritize in the years ahead?

## Agenda

* Dinner and pairing by chef Paul Ragan of AltaEats
* Interactive session to test new co-design and future scenario design methods
* Feedback and open conversation

## Attend

Interested in joining us? Email [reframe@interactivism.com](mailto:reframe@interactivism.com) with your name and a brief note on why you'd like to attend.
