## The Platform

Periodical took the hassle out of distributing and monetizing digital content, so that publishers could focus on what they loved: creating content. The platform was a CMS with integrated subscription payment processing, subscriber management, and analytics — built to publish once and distribute automatically to the responsive web, iOS, Kindle, and Android.

## Our Work

Interactivism designed the UX, visual design, and branding for both the responsive web experience and the iOS Newsstand app. The engagement covered the full design scope: information architecture, interaction design, identity guidelines and logo, UI design, and a UI component library that ensured a consistent experience across platforms.

## The Result

Publishers using Periodical could focus entirely on their editorial work, trusting the platform to handle the complexity of multi-platform distribution, subscription management, and monetization. The clean, editorial-quality design reinforced Periodical's positioning as a premium tool for serious independent publishers.
