## The Client

Level by April Beyer is an exclusive matchmaking service that marries traditional, relationship-centered matchmaking with today's technology. April Beyer, founder and CEO of Beyer & Company, is widely recognized as the most respected matchmaker in the market — and Level was her vision for bringing that expertise to a broader, digitally native audience.

## Our Work

Interactivism worked directly with April Beyer and her team to define the Level brand and translate it into two distinct website experiences — one tailored for male clients and one for female clients. The dual-site approach reflected the service's deeply personalized nature and allowed the messaging and visual tone to be calibrated for each audience while maintaining a unified brand identity.

## The Result

The Level brand and website gave April Beyer's new venture a digital presence that felt as premium and intentional as the service itself — modern enough to attract a new generation of clients, while grounded in the warmth and discretion that define elite matchmaking.
