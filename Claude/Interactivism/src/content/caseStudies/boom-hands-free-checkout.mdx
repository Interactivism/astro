## The Problem

Passwords are the single biggest source of friction — and fraud — in e-commerce checkout. Cart abandonment at the payment step costs retailers billions each year, and traditional authentication methods do little to improve the experience for returning customers.

## The Solution

Boom disrupted the legacy checkout experience by replacing the tired username-and-password model with a secure pin code, delivered instantly to the user's personal device. The result was a dramatically faster, more secure checkout flow that significantly increased conversion rates while simultaneously reducing fraud exposure.

## Our Role

Interactivism worked with Boom across all aspects of product design — from interaction models and user flows through illustration and final UI. We designed a mobile-first experience that made the novel authentication mechanism feel intuitive and trustworthy, ensuring users could move from cart to confirmation with minimal friction.
