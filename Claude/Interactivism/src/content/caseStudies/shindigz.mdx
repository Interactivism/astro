## The Client

Shindigz has been selling party supplies for 90 years, and e-commerce had become an increasingly important revenue channel over the most recent two decades. As the company's online business grew, the site's user experience had not kept pace — creating friction at critical moments in the purchase journey.

## Our Process

Interactivism worked with Shindigz during the first half of 2015. We began with an extensive heuristic evaluation of the existing e-commerce experience, systematically identifying usability issues across the site. That evaluation informed the redesign scope: global navigation, shopping cart and checkout, search and browse, templates for the home page, category pages, and detail pages, and Shindigz's unique personalization features.

## The Redesign

Every element of the redesign was designed with responsiveness across desktop, tablet, and mobile in mind. We developed a UI component library to ensure design consistency across the site and to give Shindigz's development team a reusable foundation for future updates. The result was a more coherent, conversion-optimized shopping experience that modernized one of e-commerce's longest-standing party supply brands.
