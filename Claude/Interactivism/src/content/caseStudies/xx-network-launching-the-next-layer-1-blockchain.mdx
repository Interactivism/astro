## The Engagement

Interactivism joined David Chaum and the Elixxir team as the strategic and tactical UX launch partner for xxnetwork, a new layer 1 blockchain. Embedded within the high-paced global team, we acted as the in-house UX function, simultaneously driving design and development support across multiple decentralized applications.

## What We Built

<Slideshow slides={[{"image":"/images/slideshows/xx-network-launching-the-next-layer-1-blockchain/xxmessenger.png"}]} />

**xxmessenger** — We designed the launch version of this privacy-focused dApp built on the xxnetwork blockchain. The only requirement for joining is a username, removing traditional identity barriers while preserving user privacy through the network's cMix cryptography.

---

<Slideshow slides={[{"image":"/images/slideshows/xx-network-launching-the-next-layer-1-blockchain/xxnetwork.png"}]} />

**xxscan** — We designed and provided development support for the new version of xxscan, the xxnetwork block explorer. This included a new design system for visualizing the complex process of blockchain governance.

---

<Slideshow slides={[{"image":"/images/slideshows/xx-network-launching-the-next-layer-1-blockchain/website-home-1024x557.png"}]} />

**Website** — We contributed PR and marketing support in collaboration with partner agencies Moonburn, Maneuvre, and Seratonin.

---

<Slideshow slides={[{"image":"/images/slideshows/xx-network-launching-the-next-layer-1-blockchain/votexx.png"}]} />

**Vote XX** — In collaboration with xx Labs and an international team of voting experts, we worked on a concept for a quantum-resistant, intimidation-safe election application built on the xxnetwork blockchain using cMix technology.

## Outcome

xxnetwork launched on schedule in January 2022, receiving positive coverage from TechCrunch and strong community engagement on Discord and Telegram.
