## The problem

Meridian Bank was losing 60% of applicants between the identity verification step and account funding. The team attributed it to user error. Research revealed it was a trust problem — the interface looked nothing like a bank.

## What we did

Six weeks of diary studies and usability testing surfaced three root causes: an unfamiliar document upload flow, no progress indicators, and error messages written by the legal team.

## Outcome

The redesigned flow launched to 100% of new users in Q3 2024. Drop-off fell from 60% to 36% in the first 30 days.
