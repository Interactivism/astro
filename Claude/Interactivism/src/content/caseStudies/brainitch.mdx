## The Client

Brainitch helps brands engage real consumers in conversations about their products and services within messenger-type applications. Their platform generates rich data from AI-mediated chat sessions — but that data needed a home where brand clients could make sense of it and act on it.

## The Challenge

Brainitch needed a web-based dashboard that could ingest data from the company's existing iOS client and surface it in a way that allowed brand managers to run and optimize campaigns. The dashboard needed to handle a diverse data set: polling results, psychometric profiles, and text analytics from thousands of chat sessions.

## What We Built

Interactivism designed the full dashboard experience — from information architecture and user flows through visual design and a component library. The interface collected, visualized, and segmented campaign data, psychometrics, and chat analytics, giving Brainitch clients a clear view of how consumers were responding to their AI-driven conversations. The UI library ensured the product could scale consistently as new analytics features were added.
