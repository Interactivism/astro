Ground-up redesign of a legacy network command-and-control product into OpsCenter, and the design system now powering Kratos's product portfolio across the US and Europe.

### The Client

Kratos Defense & Security Solutions (NASDAQ: KTOS) is a leading national security solutions provider, with a substantial business serving satellite operators, ground-as-a-service providers, and government and commercial customers across the space industry. Its software products manage the networks and ground systems that keep satellites — and the services that depend on them — operational.

### The Engagement

Interactivism has worked with Kratos continuously since 2018, supporting product teams across multiple business units in the US and Europe. The engagement began with a single product redesign and has grown into a long-running partnership spanning design system stewardship and product design work across a portfolio of network management, monitoring, and space domain awareness products.

### The Challenge

Compass was a true legacy product — a network command-and-control application built in the 1990s, delivered as a thick software client. It had served Kratos and its customers for decades, but the world around it had changed. Networks had grown exponentially in scale and complexity; network functions were being virtualized; and NOC teams were now structured around tiered operations with distinct workflows for monitoring, triage, analysis, and reporting. The product could no longer keep up — visually, architecturally, or operationally.

Kratos needed a ground-up redesign that would carry the product into the next era of satellite ground systems.

### What We Built

#### OpsCenter

From August 2018 through January 2019, Interactivism led the ground-up redesign of Compass. The objectives were clear:

* Modernize the look and feel for an enterprise audience
* Move from a thick software client to a web application
* Support NOC operators in identifying, triaging, and resolving issues on networks of ever-increasing scale and complexity
* Support tiered NOC operations, with distinct workflows for different operator roles, as well as analysis and reporting use cases

In 2020, Kratos consolidated its software-defined ground systems strategy under a new platform brand — OpenSpace — and rebranded the redesigned Compass as OpsCenter, the unified management layer for the platform. Interactivism has continued to partner with the OpsCenter team ever since, evolving and extending the product as its scope has grown: from managing physical and virtual network components, to carrier management, to satellite Command & Control — a single pane of glass across carriers and satellite C2, unified by a common API, user interface, and data model.

#### OpenSpace Design System (OSDS)

What started as a UI for a single product became the foundation for a design system spanning the Kratos portfolio. The OpenSpace Design System (OSDS) is delivered as a Figma library and documented on an internal Jekyll-based reference site. Kratos owns the system; Interactivism maintains the Figma library and continues to evolve it as new product requirements surface.

### Outcome

OSDS is now the standard UI across the Kratos product portfolio. What began as the redesign of a single legacy application has become the visual and interaction language for the company's software-defined ground systems — supporting operators, analysts, and decision-makers on the networks behind some of the most critical satellite services in operation today.

With the help of OSDS, Interactivism now serves two kinds of product work across the portfolio:

* **Modernizing legacy products** — applying OSDS to the redesign and continued evolution of products
* **Designing net-new products** — applying OSDS to net new products

In both cases, Interactivism partners directly with Kratos product teams — providing design services, conducting user research, and shipping production-ready designs alongside engineering teams.

The engagement is ongoing.
