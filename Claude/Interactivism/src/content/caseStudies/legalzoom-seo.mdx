## The Project

LegalZoom is the nation's leading provider of personalized online legal solutions and documents for small businesses and families. As the company rolled out a new style guide, they needed their high-traffic SEO pages brought into alignment — without sacrificing the page performance that drove significant organic revenue.

## What We Did

Interactivism redesigned the templates used across LegalZoom's SEO pages, balancing two objectives simultaneously: bringing the pages into full visual alignment with the new style guide, and maximizing SEO performance through responsive design and a tightened content strategy. Every layout decision was made with both aesthetics and discoverability in mind.

## Results

The redesigned SEO templates delivered strong year-over-year results: a 67% increase in revenue and a 298% increase in average time on page — demonstrating that good design and strong search performance are complementary, not competing, goals.
