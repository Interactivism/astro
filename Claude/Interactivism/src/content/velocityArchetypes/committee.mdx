moves:
  - title: "Name a decider for every open decision"
    body: "Not a committee, a person. They still consult everyone; they just do not need consensus to close it. Write the name somewhere the team can see it."
  - title: "Put an expiry date on open decisions"
    body: "If it is still open on that date, the decider calls it with whatever information exists. Most of the missing information was never going to arrive."
  - title: "Separate “I disagree” from “I block”"
    body: "Make it explicit that disagreement gets recorded, not resolved. People stop fighting to the last inch once they know their objection is on the record either way."
