tag: X
strengthCopy: "Your team can do something that is genuinely hard to hire for: hold a system together under pressure and ship a fix that works. In a real outage, you are the team anyone would want."
failureModeCopy: "The trouble is that firefighting crowds out the work that prevents fires. Planned work gets deferred, while the underlying causes stay where they are, and there are always more fires. The loop is self-reinforcing and it does not break on its own. It breaks when someone protects time that the next escalation is not allowed to touch."
moves:
  - title: "Protect one day a week, and defend it"
    body: "Not 20% spread across the week, which evaporates. One day, the same day, where escalations go to whoever is on rotation and everyone else works on prevention."
  - title: "Write down what caused the last five fires"
    body: "Not a formal postmortem. Five lines. The pattern is usually obvious once they sit next to each other, and two or three causes normally account for most of them."
  - title: "Give the rotation the authority to say no"
    body: "Whoever is on call decides what counts as an emergency. If anyone can declare one, the rotation is not doing its job."
