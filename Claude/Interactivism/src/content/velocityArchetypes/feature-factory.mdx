---
title: Feature Factory
tag: F
strengthCopy: >-
  You have something most teams never build: a machine that reliably turns
  decisions into shipped software. The constraint on your product
  isn’t throughput, and that puts you ahead of most of the companies you compete
  with.
failureModeCopy: >-
  The trouble with a machine that ships is that it rewards the decision to build
  over the decision to stop. Features accumulate faster than anyone removes
  them, so the surface area grows every quarter while the number of things a
  user can actually find stays flat. Eventually the product is large and the
  experience is thin, and nobody can point to the moment it happened.
moves:
  - title: Put a removal on every roadmap
    body: >-
      Commit to deleting or merging one feature a quarter, with the same
      ceremony you give a launch. The first one is the hardest, and it is the
      one that proves it is allowed.
  - title: Measure adoption 30 days out, not on launch day
    body: >-
      Track what share of the intended audience used each feature a month after
      release. Two or three releases in, you will have a list of removal
      candidates that nobody can argue with.
  - title: Make “what does this replace?” a required field
    body: >-
      Add it to the spec template. It will not always have an answer, but asking
      before the build is far cheaper than asking after.
