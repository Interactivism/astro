Hi Cameron,

A thought that's specific to Magellan AI's category: the value of an advertising intelligence platform is in the decisions it enables — but if buyers have to do significant mental work to get from data to decision, the product is working against itself.

At tvScientific we spent a lot of time on exactly this: how do you surface campaign intelligence in a way that's actionable for a media planner, not just legible to an analyst? The answer was usually about progressive disclosure — showing enough to orient, with the depth available on demand.

Intelligence tools have a narrower design vocabulary than full ad platforms, but getting that vocabulary right matters a lot for retention.
