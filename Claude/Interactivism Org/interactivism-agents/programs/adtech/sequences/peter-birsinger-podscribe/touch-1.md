Erik Wingren — co-founder & partner at Interactivism.

With Podscribe's headcount up 83% over the past two years, it looks like attribution for podcasting is moving from "emerging capability" to an industry baseline — and fast.

Attribution platforms have a specific design challenge: the data is the product, but the interface either makes it feel like insight or like homework. Advertisers and agency buyers who can't quickly read what a measurement platform is telling them don't trust it — they go back to whatever they were doing before.

We spent the last six years designing tvScientific's platform from inception — including dashboards, reporting surfaces, and campaign creation flows — through five funding rounds and 120+ people, and the Pinterest acquisition in February. The attribution measurement surface was a recurring design focus. More here: https://interactivism.com/work/tvscientific-bringing-performance-to-ctv-advertising/

I'd be curious how Podscribe thinks about the product design layer as you scale.

Does Podscribe ever go outside for design support?
