Hi Greg,

One thing that's specific to DOOH buying interfaces: the buyer persona is usually someone who already knows digital — search, social, programmatic display — and is now being asked to plan and buy across physical inventory they can't directly visualize.

That's a fundamentally different mental model than digital-only buying, and most DOOH platforms I've seen try to solve it by making the UI look like a DSP, which doesn't actually address the underlying problem.

We haven't worked directly in OOH, but the underlying challenge — building an interface for buyers at very different sophistication levels transacting across a new medium — is close to what we spent five years on at tvScientific for CTV.
